Daily Ledger — Guide to the new stock and ledger rules

14 November 2026  ·  For branch administrators  ·  Please read before the next business day

1. What changed

Before Now
Any cashier could add stock with no DR at any branch. A cashier cannot add stock without a paper DR. Their only DR-free route is Own production, and only for products the branch makes.
A cashier could tick “No paper DR — auto-generate” when receiving. That option is no longer offered to cashiers. The DR number printed on the delivery is entered instead.
Every product looked like it was made by the branch. You mark which products a branch actually makes. Everything else is treated as delivered.
An admin could post into a finished day without reopening it. A closed day is shut to everyone until it is reopened. Reopening is recorded.
A branch found out about a correction by noticing the numbers changed. The branch is shown a notice on its own ledger, naming the day, the person and what moved.

2. Own production — tell the system what each branch makes

Go to Branches, find the branch, and press “Products & own production” on that row. You will see a product list with two tick boxes. They mean two different things.

Column What it means
“In ledger” Show this product on the branch’s cashier ledger at all. Turning it off removes it from the ledger, the Daily Sheet and every receiving screen.
“Own produced here” (the box marked “Makes it”) This branch makes the product. A ticked product can be added by the cashier with no DR, through Own production. Anything left unticked is treated as delivered and must be received against its paper DR.
This is the task that needs your attention first.

The own-production tick is currently ON for every product at every branch, so the system still believes every branch makes everything. One branch was claiming all 217 of its products as made in-store. Until you untick the products a branch does not make, the new rule has nothing to act on.

Recommended: do one branch at a time. Untick the products that arrive on a delivery to that branch, then save. Start with the products your staff know are supplied from the commissary or Bodega.

3. No DR is only for what the branch makes

  • A cashier can no longer choose Add Stock (missed entry / variance), and can no longer tick No paper DR — auto-generate.
  • A cashier’s only DR-free route is Own production, and only for products ticked as made at that branch.
  • Everything else is received against the DR number printed on the paper delivery.
  • If a delivery was genuinely missed earlier, it is still received against its paper DR — that is the correction, not an adjustment.
  • Supervisors can still add stock without a paper DR, but only where the branch permits it: Branches → edit the branch → “No paper DR (add stock)”. Admin always can.

Why this changed. Over 60 days, cashiers booked 15,902 stock additions with no DR against only 590 deliveries received with a real DR. Most of those additions were filed as an “encoder omission”, which in practice means a delivery being entered late — and a delivery entered late still has a DR.

4. A closed day must be reopened before it can be corrected

  • No one can post into a closed day any more — not even an admin.
  • To correct a closed day: reopen it first (Reopen Day, top bar), make the correction, then close it again.
  • Reopening is recorded, and the branch is then told what changed (see section 5).
  • By default only an admin can reopen. This can be granted to supervisors in Settings → Permissions using “Allow reopening a closed day to correct it”.

5. The branch is told when a finished day is corrected

When someone from outside a branch corrects one of that branch’s finished days, a notice appears at the top of that branch’s cashier ledger for 21 days. It states:

  • which day was changed and who changed it,
  • which products moved and by how much,
  • the reason recorded, and when it was entered.

Staff no longer have to wonder why a number changed — they can see the change and ask the person named on the notice.

6. Settings → Permissions

  • New column “Resolve Variances” — lets a supervisor resolve delivery variances. It is off by default; tick it if you want supervisors to handle them.
  • “Ledger Override” now reads “Allow reopening a closed day to correct it”, for both Supervisor and Production in-charge.
  • A note under the table lists the rules that no setting can change: a cashier never adds stock without a paper DR, and a closed day stays shut until somebody with Ledger Override reopens it.

7. Before you start using this

  Ask your technical contact to run the database update for this release.
  Go through each branch and untick the products that branch does not make.
  Decide, per branch, whether supervisors may add stock without a paper DR (the branch switch).
  Decide whether supervisors may resolve delivery variances (Settings → Permissions).
  Brief your cashiers: products the branch makes go in as Own production; everything else needs its paper DR number.

8. For the production in-charge — delivery variance notices

When a cashier receives less than the delivery says and writes a reason, the production in-charge now sees that on their dashboard. They can open the notice, see the items involved, and write a reply for the admin to review and close.
