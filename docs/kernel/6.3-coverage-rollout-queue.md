## Multi-batch pin rule (established 2026-10-13, decisions 155/157)

When a module's declaration map grows in batches, each earlier suite pins ITS OWN
batch as present-and-exact (**subset pin** — its keys must exist with the exact
declaration and may not drift), and the **newest batch suite owns the exact
full-set pin**. Totality is never lost; it moves forward with the newest suite.
Pre-emptive applications: harpp slice-5 suite relaxed before slice 8; the 9a lane
prompted subset-style with 9b owning the exact-25.
